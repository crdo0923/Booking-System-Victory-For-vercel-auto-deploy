3. CONFIRMED FINDINGS

FINDING 1
Severity: HIGH
Area: Item request booking flow / TypeScript build
File: src/lib/services/bookings.ts
Lines: 210-219

Details:
The custom item-request payload parses quantity as unknown. TypeScript rejects
comparisons against numbers and rejects the resulting array passed to
createItemRequests().

Errors:
- item.quantity is of type unknown.
- quantity is not assignable to number.

Impact:
- npm run typecheck fails.
- Production build/type safety cannot be confirmed.
- The new item-request booking flow is at risk of failing CI or deployment.

Recommended action:
Validate and narrow quantity into a local number before constructing the
clean request array. Add focused tests for standard inventory items and the
"Other" custom item path.
